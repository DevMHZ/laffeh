# Laffah — How to delete your account

**Last updated:** 25 July 2026

This page explains how to permanently delete your Laffah account and the data
attached to it. It exists so you can request deletion **even if you no longer
have the app installed** or cannot sign in.

App: **Laffah**, published by Ali Alzoobi, trading as "Afdal".

---

## Option 1 — Delete it yourself, inside the app

1. Open **Laffah**
2. Go to **Settings**
3. Open the **Account** section
4. Tap **Delete account**
5. Read what will be deleted, tick **I understand this is permanent**, and
   confirm

The deletion happens **immediately**. There is no waiting period and no way to
undo it.

---

## What gets deleted

| Data | What happens |
|---|---|
| Your phone number and sign-in credentials | Permanently deleted |
| Your name and company name | Permanently deleted |
| The use cases you selected, and any "other" note | Permanently deleted |
| Your last saved location | Permanently deleted |

Deletion is immediate and irreversible. To use Laffah with an account again you
would need to create a new one.

**Note:** we never keep a location history — only a single current location per
device, which is overwritten on each use and removed entirely when you delete
your account.

---

## Data stored only on your device

Your saved routes, route drafts and app preferences are stored **on your phone**,
not on our servers. Deleting your account does not remove them, and we cannot
remove them for you. To erase them, uninstall the app or clear its storage in
your device settings.

---

## Data we may retain

We do not retain any of the deleted account data. If we are ever required by law
to retain a specific record, we will retain only what the law requires, for only
as long as it requires.
