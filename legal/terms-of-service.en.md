## 2. What the app does

Laffah helps you plan and optimise multi-stop routes: you place points on a map,
and the app proposes an efficient order in which to visit them, together with a
road path and estimated distances and times.

**Laffah is a planning tool, not a navigation system.** It does not provide
turn-by-turn guidance, live traffic, or real-time rerouting. Estimated times and
distances are approximations produced from third-party map data.
